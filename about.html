ABOUT

I started with music at age 10, playing cornet in the band through high school. After switching schools, my music career as a teen ended when the new school had no music program.

Fast forward to age 27, when I picked up the guitar as accompaniment for singing after performing a few gigs in a local band, but I no longer had a band to perform with.

Family and career obligations followed in my 30s and 40s, but playing and singing, despite all the busyness, never left me. I continued to play for myself and my family in the basement of my house while the kids grew up.

Now, 30 years later, with kids and career obligations subsided, I have begun my public music recording and performing career in earnest. Where will it go? Well, we will just have to see!

But around the world seems like a good place to start… I hope you will join me!
